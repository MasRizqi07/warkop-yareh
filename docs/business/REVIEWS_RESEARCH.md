## 1. Review Signals & Public Perception

Public reviews and Google Maps signals for Warkop Ya'reh in Surabaya reflect strong community grounding:

1. **Affordable Pricing:**  
   Consistently praised for honest, student-friendly, and accessible pricing within the Rp1 - 25.000 per person bracket.
2. **24/7 Availability:**  
   A dependable haven for night-shift workers, students pulling late nights, online transportation drivers, and groups looking for late-night "cangkrukan".
3. **Neighborhood Atmosphere:**  
   Described as casual, authentic, unpretentious, and welcoming. Visitors appreciate the unforced, communal neighborhood feel.
4. **Convenience & Accessibility:**  
   Both Jetis Kulon (Wonokromo) and Prapen (Tenggilis Mejoyo) are situated on accessible arterial routes in southern/central Surabaya.

## 2. Policy on Testimonials & Reviews in Code

1. **No Fabricated Customer Testimonials:**  
   The previous UI featured fictional reviews with names like "Sarah Developer", "Alex Founder", or stock images of Silicon Valley engineers praising "high-speed fiber" and "artisan batch brews". All such fixtures must be removed.
2. **Aggregated Review Signals:**  
   Only truthful, generalized signals (e.g. "Tempat nongkrong 24 jam favorit warga Surabaya", "Harga merakyat") may be presented on marketing pages.
3. **Legal & Privacy Compliance:**  
   Individual customer names and verbatim reviews must not be copied or stored without proper attribution and privacy compliance.
